https://github.com/Noelo-Lab/decbench/blob/fe5990c6468692b1b9bcf6bd2415eea42fa9667e/projects/cps/libopencm3.toml

options, including `-mthumb -mcpu=cortex-m4 -mfloat-abi=hard
-mfpu=fpv4-sp-d16 -march=armv7e-m+fp -g -Os -O2 -std=c99 -fno-common
-ffunction-sections -fdata-sections -fno-builtin`.
